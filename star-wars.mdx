Star Wars is an epic space opera franchise created by George Lucas. It began with the 1977 film *Star Wars* (later subtitled *Episode IV – A New Hope*) and has grown into one of the most influential media franchises in history.

## The Skywalker saga

The core nine-film saga follows the rise, fall, and redemption of the Skywalker family across three trilogies.

## The Force

The Force is an energy field that binds the galaxy together. Those sensitive to it can train as:

- **Jedi** — guardians of peace who follow the light side.
- **Sith** — practitioners of the dark side who seek power through passion and aggression.

## Beyond the films

The franchise also includes live-action series like *The Mandalorian* and *Andor*, animated series, novels, comics, and video games — all expanding the galaxy far, far away.
